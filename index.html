The problem, and the fix

Chhattisgarh has dozens of stunning sites.
Most visitors only see three.

Chitrakote, Kanger Valley and Bastar's temple circuit absorb the bulk of footfall, while equally rewarding places sit quiet. Roots2Routes closes that gap in three steps.

i.

Tell us what you're after

Waterfalls, wildlife, tribal culture, temples or offbeat trails — a traveller picks a mood and a travel radius, not a fixed itinerary.

ii.

We read live conditions

Footfall counters, park permits, weather and road status feed a capacity score for every tracked destination, updated through the day.

iii.

We route, not just recommend

When a favourite site nears capacity, we surface a genuinely comparable alternative nearby — same mood, shorter queue, better experience.
